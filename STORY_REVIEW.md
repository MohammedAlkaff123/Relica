# Story implementation review

The requested directors’ code and content pass is implemented. Start with [DIRECTORS_HANDOFF.md](DIRECTORS_HANDOFF.md) for task-by-task status and teammate dependencies, and [TESTLOG.md](TESTLOG.md) for observed tests.

The central change is that each machine performs a mission: lunar sample transport, Opportunity’s three water-evidence investigations, Viking imaging and soil biology, Ingenuity scouting, and Pioneer imaging/transmission. The ending honors service beyond expectations beneath the machines’ pictures.

Viking’s conversation is a dedicated cutscene, with separate locations and a cut on each speaker change. Viking is steady; Opportunity has sway. The Moon farewell and the closing voices also follow dialogue rather than racing a fixed timer. Ingenuity’s controllable final struggle leads to a single hard landing, then the damaged-blade shot and a prompted attempt to fly again.

Historical photo tasks recreate the prominent composition of PIA20285, PIA21708 and PIA10738, then fade into the real credited image. Pioneer also reveals its real Jupiter encounter sequence. These low-poly scenes are interpretive recreations; they are not surveyed terrain or exact photogrammetry.
