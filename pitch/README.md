## Short answers for the jury

**Where is NASA data used?** Each world's gravity from the NASA fact sheets drives the physics and the on-screen world panel. Mission records drive the dates, counters and discovery cards. Original NASA photos are revealed after the photo tasks and on the final wall, with their credit.

**How are facts checked?** Every fact in `content/facts.json` carries the link of the NASA page it was checked against. For example, Opportunity's 2004 water evidence is kept separate from its 2016–2017 photographs, and the discovery is *past* liquid water, not water on the surface today.

**What is real and what is invented?** Mission records and credited photographs are real. Faces, speech, conversations across missions, short routes and simplified controls are dramatized. Pioneer 10's part in the final conversation is fiction.

**Why a game?** The player gathers evidence, chooses a safe route, frames a camera and loses solar power. These actions give the historical facts consequences the player can see.

**Who is it for?** Curious young players and teachers: one chapter and its linked sources fit a single lesson.
