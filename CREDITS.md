## NASA data

### Mission facts

Each number, date and claim in the game links to the NASA page it was checked against:

- World gravity, temperature and air: [content/facts.json](content/facts.json) `worlds`, from the NASA Planetary Fact Sheets ([Earth](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html), [Moon](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html), [Mars](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/marsfact.html)).
- Mission dates, lifetimes and events: [content/facts.json](content/facts.json) `missions`, each with its `source` link.
- Discovery cards: [content/discoveries.json](content/discoveries.json), each with its `source` link.
- Main NASA pages: [Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/apollo_lrv.html), [Apollo 17 fender repair (Apollo Lunar Surface Journal)](https://www.hq.nasa.gov/alsj/a17/A17NewFender.html), [Opportunity](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mer-opportunity/), [Opportunity's last message](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/opportunitys-last-message/), [Mineral tells tale of watery past](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/mineral-tells-tale-of-watery-past/), [Viking 1](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viking-1/), [Ingenuity Mars Helicopter](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/ingenuity-mars-helicopter/), [Pioneer 10](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/pioneer-10/).

### 3D models

From [NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), simplified by the team (textures, flags and logos removed): Opportunity, Ingenuity, Viking Lander, Pioneer 10 and the astronaut.
The Lunar Roving Vehicle, the Lunar Module and Perseverance are built by the team from simple shapes, after NASA photographs ([tools/models/](tools/models/)).
Details per file: [public/models/README.md](public/models/README.md).
